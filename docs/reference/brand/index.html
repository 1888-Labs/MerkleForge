Brand assets

The MerkleForge mark is the Forged block: a solid rounded block with a Merkle root and two branches cut out of it. It reads as a tree, an anvil-struck block, and a sealed hash at once.

Files

All assets live in assets/brand/ in the repository. Wordmark text is converted to outlines, so the SVGs need no fonts.

Type

  • Space Grotesk: wordmark (Merkle 700 / Forge 400, tracking −3%), headings 500–700.
  • JetBrains Mono: code, hashes, technical labels (400–500).

Both are free on Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License.

Usage

  • Clear space: keep at least ¼ of the mark's height empty on every side.
  • Minimum size: mark 16 px; horizontal lockup 24 px tall.
  • Use the black mark on light grounds and the white mark on dark grounds.
  • Don't recolour, rotate, outline, stretch, add shadows or gradients, or rearrange the mark and wordmark.
  • Don't place the mark on busy photos; use a solid black or white panel.
